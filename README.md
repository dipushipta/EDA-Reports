# 📊 EDA Reports — Exploratory Data Analysis Projects

A collection of EDA reports performed on real-world datasets to uncover trends, patterns, and actionable business insights.

## 📁 Projects

### 1. Lighting Sales EDA
- Analyzed a dataset of 5,000+ lighting product sales records
- Identified seasonal trends and top-performing product categories
- Delivered insights to guide inventory planning and sales strategy
- **Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

### 2. Clothing Shop Website EDA
- Explored customer behavior and sales data from an e-commerce clothing store
- Analyzed traffic patterns, conversion trends, and product performance
- Generated visual reports with actionable recommendations
- **Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
